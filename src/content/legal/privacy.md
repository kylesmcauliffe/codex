Artometrics ("we", "us") operates Twilda. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

### Information we collect

- **Account data:** email address, display name, and authentication identifiers when you sign up (including via Google).
- **Content:** novels, chapters, scenes, Codex entries, snippets, and related workspace data you create in the app.
- **Usage data:** technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

### How we use information

We use your information to provide Twilda, sync your work across devices, and respond to support requests.

### Storage and security

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security. Access is restricted to your account. We use industry-standard practices to protect data in transit and at rest.

### Third parties

We use Supabase (auth and database) and Netlify (hosting). Each provider operates under its own privacy terms.

### Your rights

You may access, export, or delete your account data from your account settings.

### Retention

We retain account and content data while your account is active. Deleted accounts are purged within a reasonable period except where retention is required by law.

### Contact

Privacy questions: contact Artometrics via your account operator email.
